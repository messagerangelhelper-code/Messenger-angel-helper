Don’t Deceive Yourself

An Outreach Appeal for Healing, Restoration & Walking With Christ

“For the love of Christ constraineth us.” — 2 Corinthians 5:14

I am not sharing this message to condemn you. I am sharing it because I care about your soul, and because sometimes love requires us to speak the truth.

“Love covereth a multitude of sins.” — 1 Peter 4:8

But love does not mean that we ignore sin, justify destructive behavior, or pretend that everything is okay.

Follow Christ

“If any man serve me, let him follow me.” — John 12:26

There is a road that God calls us to walk—the highway of holiness.

“And an highway shall be there, and a way, and it shall be called The way of holiness.” — Isaiah 35:8
“Be ye holy; for I am holy.” — 1 Peter 1:16
“If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.” — Matthew 16:24

Examine the Fruit

Are you deceiving yourself about a pattern of behavior that God has been trying to bring to your attention?

If you have made it a habit to judge people unrighteously, remember:

“Judge not, that ye be not judged.” — Matthew 7:1

If you continually allow unwholesome communication, gossip, tearing people down, or corrupt words to come out of your mouth:

“Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying.” — Ephesians 4:29

Proverbs also warns about things God hates, including pride, lying, wicked intentions, false witness, and sowing discord among others.

Proverbs 6:16–19

It is time to get wisdom and understanding.

Ask yourself: What kind of fruit is this producing in my life?

  • Does it produce love?
  • Does it produce holiness?
  • Does it produce peace and forgiveness?
  • Does it point people toward Christ?

Or is it producing division, gossip, judgment, bitterness, pride, and destruction?

Nothing Is Hidden From God

“For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing.” — Ecclesiastes 12:14

Nothing is hidden from the eyes of God.

“Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.” — Amos 3:7

This is not about me being better than you. This is about all of us examining ourselves before God.

Take a Righteous Step Toward Healing

If you recognize that some of these behaviors have become a pattern in your life, don't simply accept them as “that's just who I am.” There may be wounds that need healing and patterns that need to be broken. You may need wisdom, accountability, prayer, restoration, and a renewed walk with Christ.

“The Lord is... not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.” — 2 Peter 3:9

There is still an opportunity to turn around. There is still an opportunity to repent. There is still an opportunity to heal. There is still an opportunity to follow Christ.

Don't run from the light. Let the light expose what needs to be healed.
